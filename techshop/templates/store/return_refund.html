Returns & Refunds

Our commitment to your complete satisfaction.

1. The Premium Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with your premium purchase. If an item does not meet your expectations, we accept returns within 30 days of delivery.

2. Eligibility for Returns

To ensure the integrity of our collection, returned items must be:

  • Unused and in the exact condition you received them
  • In the original, undamaged premium packaging
  • Accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase

3. Non-Returnable Items

To maintain our standards, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Software products that have been downloaded or activated
  • Customized, engraved, or personalized bespoke products
  • Products marked explicitly as final sale

4. Refund Process

Once we receive and authenticate your returned item, we will process your refund.

  • Approved refunds: Processed within 3-5 business days of inspection
  • Refund method: Credited back to your original payment method
  • Shipping costs: Original premium shipping fees are non-refundable

5. Exchanges

If you wish to exchange an item for a different variant, we recommend returning the original item for a refund and placing a new order to ensure availability.

6. Return Shipping

For standard returns, you are responsible for secure return shipping. We strongly advise using an insured, trackable service. If the return is due to a defect on our part, we will provide a complimentary prepaid label.

7. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our concierge team at concierge@premiumtech.com
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Receive your personalized Return Authorization (RMA) and instructions
  4. Carefully package and ship the item back to our facility
